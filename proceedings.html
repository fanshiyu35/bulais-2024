Editorial Note

The 2024 volume gathers six peer-reviewed full papers presented at the BulAIS workshop in Sofia on 25–26 October 2024. Each contribution was evaluated by three independent reviewers from the international programme committee, returned to its authors for revision, and accepted for publication only after the editors had verified that the reviewer comments were substantively addressed. The volume is grouped into three thematic sessions: Financial & Accounting Information Systems, Cybersecurity & E-Government, and Enterprise Systems & Innovation.

We are grateful to the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics of Sofia University “St. Kliment Ohridski” for hosting the workshop, to BAIT for industry liaison support, and to the international programme committee for its careful and pedagogically generous reviewing work.
