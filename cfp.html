Call for Papers

BulAIS 2024 invites original, peer-reviewed full papers on information-systems research with a particular openness to scholarship from Bulgaria, the Western Balkans and the broader Eastern-European IS community.

Theme

“Digital Innovation: Foundations, Frictions and Frontiers.” The 2024 edition welcomes work that interrogates how the practical innovations of the past decade — cloud-native enterprise platforms, open banking, generative AI, automated audit, and e-government back-ends — settle into the actual organisational, regulatory and cultural fabric of South-East European economies. We are equally interested in foundational theoretical work on IS adoption, governance and design.

Topics of Interest

  • Accounting & Audit IS: XBRL adoption, continuous auditing, audit analytics, AI in audit judgement
  • Cybersecurity & Information Assurance: NIS2 compliance, threat intelligence in financial IS, identity-management in public-administration
  • E-Government: digital-identity wallets (EUDIW), citizen-facing service redesign, public-procurement informatics
  • Enterprise Systems: ERP localisation in CEE/Balkan markets, two-tier ERP architectures, cloud-migration trajectories
  • Fintech & Open Banking: PSD2/PSD3, instant-payment infrastructures, regional fintech ecosystems
  • Data Governance & Trustworthy AI: GDPR-by-design, EU AI-Act implications, algorithmic accountability
  • IS Education & Capacity-Building: curriculum reform, digital-skills training, doctoral-research methods
  • Heritage Informatics: digitisation of Cyrillic manuscripts, museum information systems, digital humanities
  • Industry 4.0/5.0: smart-manufacturing in Balkan SMEs, IIoT data architectures, sustainable production IS
  • Behavioural & Socio-Technical IS: adoption, resistance, work redesign, hybrid organisational forms

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must follow the CEUR-WS one-column template (CEURART), in LaTeX or Word.
  • Submissions are double-blind: please remove author names, affiliations and any obvious self-citations from the manuscript.
  • Submit via EasyChair: easychair.org/conferences/?conf=bulais2024
  • Working language is English; all papers must be linguistically clear and grammatically correct.
  • At least one author of an accepted paper must register and present in person at the Sofia workshop.
