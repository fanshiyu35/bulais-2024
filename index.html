About the Workshop

The Bulgarian Association for Information Systems (БАИС / BulAIS), the official Bulgaria Chapter of the Association for Information Systems, holds its 2024 workshop at a moment of significant transition for the IS field in South-East Europe. Enterprises across the region are negotiating cloud-first procurement, EU NIS2 compliance, accelerated digital-banking adoption, and the practical absorption of generative AI into accounting, audit and public-administration workflows. The workshop is a deliberately compact, peer-reviewed forum — designed for substantive scholarly exchange rather than spectacle.

BulAIS 2024 is hosted by the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics (FMI) of Sofia University “St. Kliment Ohridski”, the oldest higher-education institution in Bulgaria and the principal training ground for the country's IS, software-engineering and applied-mathematics communities. The workshop continues the BulAIS tradition of pairing rigorous empirical research with a strong regional voice.

Themes of the 2024 Edition

  • Digital innovation in accounting, audit and financial information systems
  • Cybersecurity, NIS2, and information-assurance research
  • E-government and public-administration digitalisation in the Western Balkans
  • Cloud, edge and federated architectures for SME ERP
  • Behavioural and socio-technical perspectives on IS adoption
  • Open-banking, PSD2/PSD3, and fintech information systems
  • Data governance, GDPR, and trustworthy AI in public services
  • Digital-skills capacity-building and IS education in CEE
  • Industry 4.0 / 5.0 applications in regional manufacturing
  • Heritage informatics and Cyrillic-script digital humanities
Held under the auspices of BulAIS — Българската асоциация за информационни системи, the official Bulgaria Chapter of the Association for Information Systems (AIS). BulAIS promotes IS scholarship through the Balkans via annual workshops, doctoral consortia, and joint curricular work with member faculties.
